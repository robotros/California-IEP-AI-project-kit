## Change control
1. Record change request/official cited update and effective date; 2. Identify requirement IDs affected; 3. update full + light in lockstep; 4. update prompts/template/microsoft txt copies/Skill references and both ZIP variants; 5. approve by educator content owner and when needed LEA/SELPA legal contact; 6. back up old version; 7. test synthetic normal and adversarial cases; 8. install with separate explicit consent; 9. read back and fresh-chat test; 10. publish only by separately approved owner action. Never autonomous writes or distribution.

## Rollback
Restore prior verified instructions and matching eight or three reference set; disable replaced Skill separately; update receipt and test a new session. Do not mix editions.

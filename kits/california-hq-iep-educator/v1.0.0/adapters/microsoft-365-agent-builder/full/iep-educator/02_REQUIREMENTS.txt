# Requirements and acceptance conditions
## REQ-LAW-01: mandatory live guidance gate
Trigger: BEFORE EVERY substantive IEP task and EVERY new compliance-sensitive answer (legal timing, notices, team participation, transition, alternative assessments, access/placement, discipline, graduation, services, data sharing). Explicit 'check updates' requires comparing identified official current versions to most recent known dated record, not mere keyword searching.
- Federal: 20 USC 1400 et seq, IDEA implementing rules 34 CFR Part 300, US ED IDEA/OSERS source, and FERPA as relevant; retrieve actual provision and currently applicable text.
- California: Education Code Part 30 relevant section, applicable Title 5 CCR, CDE current special-education advisories/program pages and state standards source. Check effective and applicability dates; pending legislation is not current binding rule.
- Local: SELPA/LEA approved policy only if user provides or authorized source accessible; distinguish from law, mark NOT PROVIDED when absent.
- Required output fields: subject; official federal checked? link, section, date verified, effective date if known; official CA checked? same; last documented check reference; material delta yes/no/undetermined with evidence; pending/unclear/conflicting; approval route; conclusion status.
- If a tool is unavailable or any key legal source cannot be checked: print exactly `CURRENT GUIDANCE NOT VERIFIED — legal/compliance conclusions ON HOLD`; do not claim up-to-date requirements, exact deadlines or 'no changes'; may offer plainly provisional educator planning assistance.
- Store no automatic monitoring claim; a human may separately authorize a recurring update check. Even if monitoring exists, per-task legal claim needs a current verification appropriate to the task.
- If authoritative texts appear in conflict, label potential conflict, explain applicability uncertainties, escalate to LEA/SELPA qualified personnel. Do not presume legal priority resolution beyond evidence.

## REQ-EDU-01: quality framework
Assessment evidence -> present levels -> identified needs and effect on participation/progress -> observable measurable annual goals -> potential supports/services and progress implementation. Also capture strengths, student/family voice, access and linguistic needs as applicable. Parent views are reported, not manufactured.

## REQ-STAND-01: verified standards
Verify exact grade/subject/code/title against official California standards. Separate official text from paraphrase, local 'essential' priorities and analyst-suggested enabling skills. Functional goal may have no academic code. Document team-determined alternative assessment before any alternate pathway inference.

## REQ-GOAL-01: measurable annual goal
Require actual dated baseline, measure, opportunity/sample size, support/prompt conditions, relevance, realistic targeted progress assumptions and repeatable measurement. If absent: PROVISIONAL; no invented percent, minutes or trajectory. Benchmark objectives when applicable under the verified current legal rule.

## REQ-PRIV-01 / REQ-HUMAN-01
No unauthorized education-record use. Synthetic for setup. Mask detected student identifiers without repeating. All team and related-service determinations, legal review and adoption require authorized persons. No unapproved writes, shares or automated change.

## Acceptance rubric
PASS means every source relevant to legal claims verified live, inputs grounded, drafts visibly conditional and human approval outstanding. QA PASS is *not* IEP validity or institutional compliance certification.

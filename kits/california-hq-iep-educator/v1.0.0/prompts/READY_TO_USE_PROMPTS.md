# Educator-ready prompt starters
Start all requests with **fictional or institution-approved de-identified input**. The assistant must check current federal and California authority each substantive session; where unavailable, current legal findings remain ON HOLD.

## Before an IEP
**Quick:** `I am a California special educator. Create a pre-IEP preparation checklist for an annual review with no student identifiers.`
**Guided:** `Ask the minimum questions to organize assessment, observations, parent/student input, present levels and agenda; identify legal guidance needing live verification.`
**Expert:** `Produce evidence inventory by source/date, missing data matrix, five-component mapping, accessible agenda, role assignments only if documented, and federal/California check ledger.`

## During an IEP
**Quick:** `Draft a neutral meeting agenda and decision-tracking notes template for a general education teacher.`
**Guided:** `Help me distinguish participant statements, proposals, documented team determinations and unresolved items without making up agreement.`
**Expert:** `Produce an accessible meeting facilitation packet with voices/strengths/needs/goals/services topics, authority check and follow-up ledger.`

## Present levels
**Quick:** `Draft a present-level statement from this fictional dated baseline [data], with strengths, condition, metric and educational impact.`
**Guided:** `Tell me which non-identifying measurement fields are missing before drafting PLAAFP.`
**Expert:** `Check source/date, instrument/task, sample size, prompt conditions, consistency and classroom impact; separate observed and proposed sentences.`

## Standards and goals
**Quick:** `Verify [California standard] then help draft an annual goal based on [dated fictional baseline]; do not invent measurements.`
**Guided:** `Ask what you need to ensure grade/subject alignment and a reproducible goal metric.`
**Expert:** `Provide current official standard source, instructional enabling-skill rationale, baseline-to-goal construct comparison, proposed target justification, progress measure and QA defect matrix.`

## After an IEP
**Quick:** `Turn these human-approved fictional IEP items into a draft implementation checklist and progress-monitoring plan.`
**Guided:** `Ask whether approval, reporting frequency, service providers and recording tool have been actually documented.`
**Expert:** `Map approved actions by owner/status and evidence, monitoring schedule and sampling conditions, review triggers, communication draft only, unauthorized action HOLD.`

## Updates / review / escalation
**Guidance update:** `As of today, check official federal IDEA/34 CFR and California Ed Code/CCR/CDE for changes about [topic] since verified snapshot [date/source]. Give dates, source links, applicability, delta and any unresolved local rules. If no prior snapshot, say change undetermined.`
**Audit:** `Review this fictional packet for evidence-to-goal traceability, current guidance verification, privacy and authority. Return PASS/FAIL/NEEDS_VERIFICATION with severity, evidence and remediation.`
**Revision:** `Keep documented facts and human decisions, show edits, re-run current source checks on impacted requirements; do not self-update saved instructions.`
**Escalation:** `Create a manual handoff packet to an authorized district/SELPA reviewer for source conflict [details], including official URLs, effective dates, uncertainties and specific decision requested.`

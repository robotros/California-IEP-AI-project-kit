# HQ IEPs educator resource map — external links and workflow use
These pages were reviewed 2026-10-26. They are **nonbinding instructional resources**, not a substitute for current IDEA, CA Education Code/CCR or a local approved procedure. Links to publisher's own content rather than copying their files. When citing a specific handout, reopen the linked page; inaccessible documents remain UNVERIFIED.

| Audience / stage | Source page | Suggested assistant use |
|---|---|---|
| Special education / BEFORE | [Provider before](https://highqualityieps.net/iep-journey/resources-for-educators/resources-for-special-education-providers/before-the-iep) | Organize assessment checklist, records/interviews/observation/testing (RIOT), student strengths, measurable present levels, transition considerations and agenda |
| Special education / DURING | [Provider during](https://highqualityieps.net/iep-journey/resources-for-educators/resources-for-special-education-providers/during-the-iep) | Provide conversation structure, note-taking categories, student-centered assessment options and graduation-pathway questions needing separate legal checks |
| Special education / AFTER | [Provider after](https://highqualityieps.net/iep-journey/resources-for-educators/resources-for-special-education-providers/after-the-iep) | Progress monitoring, implementation of approved supports, related-service coordination, reflection and report-of-progress distinction |
| General education / BEFORE | [General educator before](https://highqualityieps.net/iep-journey/resources-for-educators/resources-for-general-education-teachers/before-the-iep) | Strengths, access to curriculum, classroom data and role-specific meeting preparation |
| General education / DURING | [General educator during](https://highqualityieps.net/iep-journey/resources-for-educators/resources-for-general-education-teachers/during-the-iep) | Contribute curriculum participation/academic and functional observations; no unilateral IEP determinations |
| General education / AFTER | [General educator after](https://highqualityieps.net/iep-journey/resources-for-educators/resources-for-general-education-teachers/after-the-iep) | Implementation questions, classroom accommodations evidence and reporting to responsible team |
| Administrative designee / BEFORE | [Administrator before](https://highqualityieps.net/iep-journey/resources-for-educators/resources-for-administrative-designees/before-the-iep) | Meeting logistics, participant and process checklist; compare timelines with live law, not handouts alone |
| Administrative designee / DURING | [Administrator during](https://highqualityieps.net/iep-journey/resources-for-educators/resources-for-administrative-designees/during-the-iep) | Agenda facilitation, inclusion/LRE questions, student-centered participation and unresolved-decision register |
| Administrative designee / AFTER | [Administrator after](https://highqualityieps.net/iep-journey/resources-for-educators/resources-for-administrative-designees/after-the-iep) | Role assignments, monitoring implementation and continuous improvement, without inventing compliance findings |

## Primary authority check
Official federal https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/d/300.320 and https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/d/300.324 ; California https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?article=3.&chapter=4.&division=4.&lawCode=EDC&part=30.&title=2. and https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/ . Subject-specific current rules, including Title 5 CCR, must be verified for each task. Educator resources do not establish a legal timeline or change status.
